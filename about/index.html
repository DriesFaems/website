About Prof. Dr. Dries Faems

Chair of Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Technological Transformation at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, and a recognized GenAI expert on artificial intelligence business applications.

His work combines academic research on innovation management and entrepreneurship with practical experimentation in generative AI. He helps organizations leverage generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Gemini to drive business transformation and innovation, and he leads WHU's partnership with OpenAI.

Prof. Faems' academic research has contributed to advancing the understanding of innovation management and entrepreneurship, with over 6,000 citations and publications in leading journals such as the Academy of Management Journal, Strategic Management Journal, Journal of Management, Research Policy and Journal of Management Studies.

Through the GenAI Nerd Channel on YouTube, he provides free, practical content about new generative AI technologies and their business applications, making cutting-edge AI knowledge accessible to executives, entrepreneurs and business professionals.

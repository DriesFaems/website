About Prof. Dr. Dries Faems

Prof. Dr. Dries Faems is Chair of Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Technological Transformation at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management. He is a recognized GenAI expert and leading authority on artificial intelligence business applications.

What does Dries Faems do?

His work combines academic research on innovation management and entrepreneurship with practical experimentation in generative AI. He helps organizations leverage generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Gemini to drive business transformation and innovation, and he leads WHU's partnership with OpenAI.

What is his academic background?

Prof. Faems holds the Chair of Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Technological Transformation at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management. His academic research has contributed to advancing the understanding of innovation management and entrepreneurship, with over 6,000 citations and publications in leading journals such as the Academy of Management Journal, Strategic Management Journal, Journal of Management, Research Policy and Journal of Management Studies.

What is the GenAI Nerd Channel?

The GenAI Nerd Channel is a YouTube channel run by Prof. Faems that provides free, practical content about new generative AI technologies and their business applications, making cutting-edge AI knowledge accessible to executives, entrepreneurs and business professionals.

Does he offer AI workshops and training?

Yes. Prof. Faems provides hands-on GenAI workshops and executive education programs, including an Online GenAI Bootcamp and customized corporate AI workshops. Learn more on the GenAI Workshops page.

Where can I find his research?

His research covers innovation management, entrepreneurship, collaboration for innovation and technological transformation. Learn more on the Research page or view his complete publication record on Google Scholar.

GenAI Workshops for Companies and Executives

Prof. Dr. Dries Faems provides hands-on generative AI workshops and training programs for executives, managers, entrepreneurs and business professionals.

The focus is not simply on explaining artificial intelligence. Participants work directly with leading generative AI tools and explore how they can be applied to real business problems, organizational processes, innovation and strategy.

What do participants learn in the GenAI workshops?

Workshops combine an understanding of current developments in generative AI with practical experimentation. Topics include ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Gemini, prompt engineering, AI-supported workflows, AI agents, innovation applications and approaches to implementing generative AI inside organizations.

Who are the workshops designed for?

The programs are primarily designed for executives, managers, entrepreneurs and other business professionals who want to understand how generative AI can improve the way they and their organizations work.

No coding experience is required. The emphasis is on practical business applications rather than software development.

Can a GenAI workshop be customized for a company?

Yes. Corporate GenAI workshops can be adapted to the specific industry, participants, strategic priorities and use cases of an organization.

Company-specific workshops can combine an introduction to recent AI developments with hands-on exercises, use-case development, prompting techniques and discussion of organizational implementation challenges.

Which generative AI tools are covered?

Training can include leading platforms such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Gemini. The emphasis is on helping participants understand the strengths and limitations of different tools and how to select them for specific business tasks.

What GenAI training formats are available?

Current activities include the Online GenAI Bootcamp, executive education programs at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, and customized corporate workshops.

Who is Prof. Dr. Dries Faems?

Prof. Dr. Dries Faems is Chair of Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Technological Transformation at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management. His work combines academic research on innovation and entrepreneurship with practical experimentation in generative AI and AI-supported business applications.

He also runs the GenAI Nerd Channel, which provides practical content about new generative AI technologies and business applications.
